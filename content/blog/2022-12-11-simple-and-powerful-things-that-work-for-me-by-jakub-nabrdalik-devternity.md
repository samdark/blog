It was a good talk about many everyday practices that help being more productive end enjoy work/life more. Jakub is DevOps.

- Count everything in money.
- Understand before implementing. Proof validity.
- Design architecture:
    - Count cognitive load.
    - Take quality requirements into account.
    - Look for alternatives. Do not immediately implement first variant.
- Design for failure.
- Always do MVPs to get early feedback.
- Refine requirements.
- Use tests to manage requirements.
- Egoless programming is a goal i.e. do not get attached to your code.
- Discarding drafts is alright.
- You learn by making mistakes.
- Logs should tell everything w/o using debugger.
- Do not rush. Stressed developer == buggy fix. First idea is usually wrong.
