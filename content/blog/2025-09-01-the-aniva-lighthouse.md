An abandoned, 31-meter tall structure on Sivuchya Rock at the southernmost point of Sakhalin Island, Russia. Built by the Japanese in 1939 due to dangerous currents and fogs, it was later converted to nuclear power by the Soviets before being fully abandoned in 2006.

Takes about 4 hours off-road drive and 1.5 hours on a boat to get there.
