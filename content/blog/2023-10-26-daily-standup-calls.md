Daily standup calls are controversial practice. Some people think these are useless, some people think these are absolutely necessary.

⭐️  From my experience these are useful if done correctly:

1. There should be **hard time limit**. 1.5—2 minutes for each participant with rare exceptions. Overall standup should not last longer than 15-20 minutes.
2. This limit, order of speaking etc. **should be moderated** and directed by a single person.
3. The same person should **write down a brief summary** for the ones who was missing.
4. **Cameras** should be on.

⭐️ What you get from standups as a lead:

1. **Know** about any blockers, not meeting deadlines etc. **in advance**. Can direct to help right there quickly or schedule another call.
2. Collect **team feedback** about everything.
3. **Feel that something is wrong about person** judging from voice/video.
4. **Know** that person is doing **bullshit job**.
5. You can bring business/sales persons so they do a quick summary as well. That **motivates the team**. They feel that their work is useful.

⭐️ What you get from standups as developer:

1. **Ability to warn** about not meeting deadlines, blocker etc. **in advance** and get help.
2. Feeling like **you're part of a team**, not just remote contractor.
3. Motivates you to **check sprint tasks** instead of doing something you prefer.
