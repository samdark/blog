Q: Have you practiced Design Review? What are advantages and disadvantages? Doesn't it add too much overhead for tech lead / architect?

A: I did at Skyeng. Highly recommended. In the end team does more in less time.

Q: How to determine what task needs design review and what does not?

A: Can assignee immediately tell about how it should be done in a list of steps? If no — you need design review.
