> I'm sorry that this was such a long letter, but I didn't have time to write you a short one
> 
> Blaise Pascal, 1657

Calls, voice messages, and long letters are all caused by inability to prepare these by making these shorter. Either because of lack of time, lack of energy, or lack of skill.

"Here's the mess I have in my head, I can not make any sense of it right now so would you please spend time on it instead?".

Note that there are exceptions (as usual). The message length could be longer to align with its purpose and/or recipient needs.
