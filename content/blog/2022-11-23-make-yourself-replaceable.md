It is very important to make yourself replaceable. That's the only way the project could survive in the long term.

I always try to set up projects this way: writing docs, getting right people to be in the team and give em full or nearly full access and responsibility.

Currently I'm writing this in a severe flu fever 🤒 but Yii3 development hasn't stopped because of it. Team is doing great.

And it works the same way not only for the OpenSource but for commercial products as well.

Choose people. Trust people. There will be mistakes but it worth it.
