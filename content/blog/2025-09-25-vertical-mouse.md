I was wondering about vertical mice for a while but never tried it before. None of my friends were using these, and getting one just to try it wasn't my thing.

With my recent hobby of 3D printing, I've stumbled upon the [Ergo Vertical Mouse V2](https://makerworld.com/en/models/484955-ergo-vertical-mouse-v2-left-and-right-handed) and decided to try it.

Printing and assembly were fun. The mouse internals are ingenious. It uses a standard "[mouse kit](https://eu.store.bambulab.com/products/wireless-mouse-components-kit-002)", which is designed for a regular mouse. The buttons, therefore, are pressed by rubber rods going through cleverly designed tunnels.

After it was done, I actually used the mouse for a day. Buttons and the wheel are far from being perfect, but I've expected it. In a non-printed mouse, that should not be an issue. The rest works wonders.

⬆️ **Advantages**

It is really comfortable. The wrist stays in the natural position. The elbow is in a way more comfortable position than when using a traditional mouse. Likely it should be very good for the ones with wrist pain.

⬇️ **Disadvantages**

It is tall. You can't quickly move your hand from the mouse to the keyboard and back. It seems these models are for the people who don't use keyboards much.

Also, when you press buttons, the mouse moves enough for the cursor to slip. Likely could be solved if the mouse is heavy.

—
Overall, since I type way more than I use the mouse, I'll stay with my [veteran Razer DeathAdder Chroma](https://t.me/samdark_blog/147).
