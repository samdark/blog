Do you have a note taking routine?

My memory is far from ideal so I make notes. Lots of notes. Usually I start with pen and paper. Then, after it's done, I re-read it, write down important parts into digital notes or TODOs and destroy the paper.

I've used Evernote for digital notes for years. Then, earlier this year I've switched to [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/) + Dropbox for syncing. Reasons:

1. Notes are markdown.
2. You own your files.
3. Good desktop and phone clients.
4. Multiple ways of structuring/searching/linking notes.

For TODOs I use [Dynalist](https://dynalist.io/).
