I bought this mouse in Norway in 2012. Since then it works flawlessly except switches that I've replaced multiple times. These are starting to produce double-clicks and skipping clicks sometimes. There's a useful web tester for that: [https://mousetester.com/](https://mousetester.com/). Luckily, these switches are easy to get and replace.

**📝 What's needed**

1. D2FC-F-7N(20M) switches.
2. A set of Feet Skates Mouse Pads.
3. Soldering iron + tweezers.

All that could be easily obtained from AliExpress.

**👉 How to do it**

1. Remove two top feet skates from the mouse.
2. Either remove bottom sticker or make a hole up from the middle of the bottom feet skate.
3. Unscrew screws.
4. Unsolder and remove old switch and put a new in place. Make it stick out a bit.
5. Assemble the mouse. Do not attach feet skates yet.
6. Test it.
7. Attach feet skates. Either the ones removed if these are OK or replacement ones of removed ones were damaged.

Done. Enjoy another 3-4 years of perfect clicking.
