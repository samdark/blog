title: "Trust people, talk to people"
date: 2023-03-10 16:20:07
edited: 2023-03-10 16:21:32

I had no trust in people for many years. I'm still not sure what caused it but until relatively recently my defaults for people except family and friends were "do not trust, avoid". I've preferred online services and forms and machines to people. Partially because I understood better how machines operate and had no idea about people.

That's similar for many IT people out there. In the past there were memes and stories about this side of our profession everywhere.

Russia was super-comfortable in this regard: awesome services, delivery of virtually anything, e-appointments, e-govt, awesome detailed maps with all the businesses and their websites etc. I was able to live in this happy bubble for years. With time and my involvement in OpenSource I've expanded the bubble borders a lot and started to talk with many IT people and sometimes even asked for info/help.

In 2022 I've moved to Armenia and it changed my defaults to "trust people until proven otherwise, talk to people whenever possible". In the country everything works through people. If you try to do things like in Russia, open a map and search for something, you see that there's basically nothing. Asking friends and even random people reveals that, on contrary, there's everything you could imagine, any of your problems could be solved, there are no dead-end situations etc.

Overall, people are great by default. If you think overwise, try talking and you'll see the difference.
