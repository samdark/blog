This fall, I’ll be speaking at [Pyh.conf’25](https://conf.phpyh.ru/?utm_source=tg&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=partners&utm_content=notice&utm_term=samdark_blog) — a new PHP conference that will take place in Moscow on September 19.

If you followed PHP Russia, you might’ve noticed we’re taking a break this year. In the meantime, Valentin Udaltsov — PHP Russia program committee member, author of the Pyh Telegram channel, organizer of four PHP meetups in Moscow, and active open source contributor — is launching something new.

The program looks solid — internals, performance, design, architecture. I’ll be there with [a talk about the internals of Yii3: how it works under the hood and where it’s headed](https://conf.phpyh.ru/program/yii3?utm_source=tg&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=partners&utm_content=notice&utm_term=samdark_blog).

I won’t be there alone — other members of the Yii3 team are also taking part. Sergey Predvoditelev (vjik) is on the program committee, and both Dmitry Derepko (xepozz) and Alexey Gagarin (roxblnfk) will be giving talks.

🎟 [Grab your tickets](https://conf.phpyh.ru/price?utm_source=tg&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=partners&utm_content=notice&utm_term=samdark_blog).
📄 [Full schedule](https://conf.phpyh.ru/program?utm_source=tg&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=partners&utm_content=notice&utm_term=samdark_blog).

See you there.
