## 💡 Useful mental models

1. Predictive
2. Usefully durable
3. Cheap / Easy to reason over (low inference cost)

"Cheap" for humans is easy to remember w/o looking it up.

## 🧠 Brain structure

- 3% is stem — automatic functions such as breathing etc.
- 75% is neocortex — to create models of the world for predicting the future.

## 🤔 Whom to hire?

- Intelligence++ — people who are good model builders.
- Reason abstractly, apply to concrete situation.
- Models should be relevant.

## Anxiety

Price of intelligence is anxiety = Adrenaline + Cortisol.

Effects: shallow breathing, rapid heart rate, **impaired ability to reason** 🤯

Anxiety comes from model invalidation i.e. inability to predict the future.

The job of a leader is to lower anxiety of his people by making it easy to predict their future.

## Top-down/central planning

Top-down/central planning (one lead decides it all) = definitive failure.

- Latency (reaction to emergent properties) — local conditions change too often.
- Throughput (# of decision to be made in big system).
- **Distributed systems perform better w/ distributed decision making and control**
- Humans are bad computers — more complicated
- Humans need agency (making decisions on their own, ability to act) — don't do "Just do the thing!" (invalidation of mental model)
- Humans do not perfectly follow instructions
- Humans feel anxiety

## Central principles/distributed decisions

- Articulate a set of durable principles and objectives that apply everywhere at all times. You, as a leader, is responsible for it.
- Measurable business objectives.
- We're going to optimize for objectives w/ constraints of principles.
- Actively and visibly demo in words and deeds you adhere to the principles and objectives.
- 90% of the time spend on explaining the WHY.
- Leaders are responsible for:
    - Principles
    - Objectives
    - Incentives
    - Tie-breaking

Your team follows your system of making decisions.

> You don't fail by failing. You fail by failing to notice.

How to notice mistake fast and fix it cheap?
Cost of the mistake should be low.

Lower anxiety by saying that failing is fine and having a plan for failure. Do not blame people for bad decision.


## QA between me and Dave Resin

> ❓ Isn't negative feedback as important?

💡 Truth is important.  Things that make their models better are important.  So... If your employee is not doing well, then you need to tell them as soon as you notice and tell them what you expect.  You need to fix their model.  They will be anxious because you just invalidated their model of the Present, but... if you tell them very clearly how to think about their job then they can quickly build a new model and move forward.
