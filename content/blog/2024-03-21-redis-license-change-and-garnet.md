While [Redis changed its license from BSD-4 to dual RSALv2+SSPLv1](https://github.com/redis/redis/pull/13157), [Microsoft open-sourced Garnet](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/blog/introducing-garnet-an-open-source-next-generation-faster-cache-store-for-accelerating-applications-and-services/). It is Redis-compatible storage that is way more performant and is MIT licensed.
