Today there was an emergency with saving data on my production system. I wasn't fully awake yet and tried to understand what's wrong — checked logs, dug  into data, drew some schema with ideas on why it might fail and which circumstances to test.

My sleepy brain was thinking it's not taking much time but it was wrong. It took two hours before our daily which isn't great timing for fixing critical stuff.

Luckily, less sleepy colleague immediately suggested to fix it in a dirty way and get to the root cause while clients can work. So we did.

**Lesson learned (again and again):** fix it somehow then fix it right.

There's one thing though. Not understanding the cause entirely may make it way worse.
