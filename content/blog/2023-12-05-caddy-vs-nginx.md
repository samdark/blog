There's [recent interest in Caddy, a reverse proxy server that could be used same as Nginx to serve PHP-FPM based app](https://php.watch/articles/caddy-php). I'm checking for even faster Nginx alternative for years and, for classic stateless PHP request-response model, nothing was able to beat Nginx so far.

[A recent benchmark](https://github.com/grn-it/nginx-vs-caddy-performance-benchmark)
