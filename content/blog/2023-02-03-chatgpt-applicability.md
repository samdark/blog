> Describe where ChatGPT is not applicable and should not be used.

ChatGPT is a highly sophisticated AI language model, but it is not a replacement for human expertise in certain areas. It should not be used:

1. In situations where human judgement is required: ChatGPT does not have the ability to make ethical or moral decisions.

2. In life-critical decision making: ChatGPT is not capable of providing accurate and trustworthy information for life-critical decisions such as medical diagnosis or treatment.

3. In place of professional expertise: ChatGPT is not a substitute for the knowledge and experience of professionals in fields such as law, finance, and engineering.

4. In financial transactions: ChatGPT should not be used for financial transactions, as it does not have the ability to understand financial markets or the consequences of financial decisions.

5. In creating or managing legal documents: ChatGPT is not capable of creating legally binding documents or providing legal advice.

It's important to use ChatGPT responsibly and within its limitations, as well as to validate any information obtained from it with other sources.
