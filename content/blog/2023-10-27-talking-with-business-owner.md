Got a question today:

*"Did you ever had a situation when you propose a good solution but it's declined and old inefficient way of doing things is used?"*

Of course I did. Usually everything just works as is. Either there's no need for change (it is cheaper to use this inefficient way than developing a solution) or business owner doesn't see the profit.

In the first case you should not do anything. In the second case you should provide numbers.

For example, there are 10 people editing Google sheets constantly doing the same stuff again and again. Right questions to get answers for:

1. What's the current cost of keeping these 10 persons? Could these be fired reducing costs or do something more valuable?
2. What's the cost of developing and supporting automated solution?
3. When would we see the difference? How much would that be monthly?

When you have answers you can speak on the business language.

⭐️ Before:

**You**: Our process is inefficient, I can automate that. That would be cool!
**Business**: Meh...


⭐️ After:

**You**: Our process is inefficient. We are keeping 10 people and paying $200 each. That's $2000 a month.

I can develop the solution in 3 weeks. That would cost 3000$ initially and $100 for support hours. That would increase costs by $1000 for one month and then reduce costs by 1900$ monthly next months if we fire these 10 people.

**Business**: Extra 1900$ is useful. Let's do it!
