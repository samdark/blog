During USSR times, the state built special studios on top stories of almost regular panel apartment houses. There are pretty much invisible if you don't know what you are looking for. The penthouses are about 5 meters in height with large windows always facing north (because this way there's consistent light comes into it). These are still used by top artists from the union of artists.

On the photo, it is a small part of my aunt's studio window.
