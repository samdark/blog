❓I was recently asked "what questions as a candidate should I ask during a team lead interview"? Here's my list:

1. Who'll be my supervisor? What are his expectations? 🔍
2. How the company makes money? 💰
3. As a lead, what rights and responsibilities will I have? 🦸‍♂️
4. Will I lead a new team or existing team? 👯
5. Would I be able to hire/fire? 🔥
6. What are current team problems? 🐞
7. Is there a process of knowledge exchange? 📕
8. Who's the most experienced lead/architect/developer? 💪
9. What plans CTO has for the next few years? 📈
10. What happened with my predecessor? 😂
