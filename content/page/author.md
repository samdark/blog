I'm Alex Makarov, engineer and IT professional with decades of experience. Currently CTO of [Twindo.ai](https://twindo.ai/). In the past worked at Wrike, [Stay.com](http://asgeir.xyz/stay), Skyeng, and more.

[Participant of various other OpenSource projects](https://github.com/samdark/).

Blogging in Russian and English since 2005. This blog is updated with all sources but for latest content and comments [check my Telegram channel](https://t.me/samdark_blog).
